📖 HOW TO FRENZY

1 THE GIMMICK

EVERY CARD IS YELLOW. No colors to match — you match by NUMBER or throw down ACTION cards.

  • Number on number → same number.
  • Top card is an ACTION → any number is legal.
  • Action cards are ALWAYS playable.
  • Empty your hand first to win.

2 ACTION CARDS

🚫 SKIPNext player loses their turn.
🔄 REVERSETurn order flips. In 2-player it bounces right back to you.
+2 PENALTYNext player draws 2 and is skipped.
+4 MEGANext player draws 4 and is skipped. Devastating.
⇄ SWAPTrade hands with a victim of your choice. Brutal when you're losing.
🔥 WILDFIREEVERYONE draws 1 card — including you.
🛡 SHIELDRaises a barrier that blocks the next time you're forced to draw (+2, +4, battle penalties, wildfire, chaos draws) — then it breaks.
⚔ BATTLEChallenges the next player to a SUDDEN CARD BATTLE. Loser draws 4. Tie → both draw 2.

3 CHAOS FRENZY METER

Every card played charges the meter by +8%. At 100% a random CHAOS EVENT detonates:

  • DRAW RAIN — everyone draws 2.
  • DECK EXPLOSION — the discard pile blasts back into the deck.
  • TOP CARD MORPH — the pile shapeshifts into a random card.

4 SUDDEN CARD BATTLE

A 3-second tap war: both players mash their side of the screen (or keyboard: left A S D F · right J K L ;). Most taps wins. The loser draws 4. A CPU opponent mashes at a rate set by its difficulty.

5 MODES

  • VS CPU — you against a bot: EASY CHAOTIC INSANE
  • LOCAL PVP — 2–4 players on one device (hot-seat). The screen hides your hand between turns: pass the device when the shield screen shows up. Mix in CPU seats if you're short on friends.

Career stats (wins, losses, streaks, battles won) are saved in your browser for every player name.
